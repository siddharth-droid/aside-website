# Aside film: three storylines

All three: 25–35 s, 16:9 at 1920×1080, 30 fps, built in code with HyperFrames. Cast from `cast.js` (Sam, Ade, Tom), the real orb from `art/orb/`. Paper-collage look shared with the site's collage chapters. Sound: music and paper sound effects made locally (free); narration either a free Microsoft neural voice (edge-tts, no key; the script text is sent to Microsoft to synthesise) or no voice with large on-screen lines.

## A. One frame, two ways (recommended: the story strangers already live)

**Device:** life before and after, one person's real task. **Form:** one collage board that keeps rebuilding itself, never cutting away.

Sam at 09:12 has to fix one sentence in an email. We watch the old way on a kraft-paper board: torn browser windows slap on top of each other, arrows loop, a ransom-letter counter ticks to "8 steps · 2 app switches", Sam's face goes from neutral to frustrated. The paper rips back to 09:12 and the same task happens again with Aside: the orb sticker slides in from the board's right edge, three keycaps stamp down, and the rewrite lands in teal in the email; Sam is relieved. Two quick echoes (Ade's error explained, Tom's form translated) use the same three stamps, then the end card.

- **First 2 s:** the email strip "Per my last email, the copy deadline was Monday…" torn and taped to kraft paper, a red marker circle drawing round it, Sam's sticker beside it, neutral.
- **Beats:** the sentence → the chat-tab trip piles up (8 steps, 2 switches) → paper rips back → orb slides in, 3 keys stamp → rewrite lands, Sam relieved → Ade, Tom echo (3 stamps each) → "Keep working. Ask Aside." + Download.
- **Look:** kraft and newsprint with halftone, torn edges, tape; ransom letters in red, yellow, blue and ink; the orb as a white-edged sticker.
- **Sound:** a light plucked-paper groove; slaps on every piece, a marker squeak, a rip, three keycap clacks.

## B. The trip

**Device:** a number lands. **Form:** one continuous take across a long paper map.

The camera travels along a hand-drawn paper map of "the trip": from the app, out to the browser, the chat tab, and back, with eight numbered footprints and two border crossings stamped "app switch". At the end the camera pulls up: a pair of scissors cuts the map, and Aside's route is a short straight line of three keycaps from the app to the answer and back.

- **First 2 s:** a dotted footprint trail starting at a paper cut-out of an email window, the first footprint stamped "01".
- **Beats:** footprints 01–08 with stamps → pull up to see the whole loop → scissors cut → three-keycap shortcut → end card.
- **Look:** a paper map with engraved lines and one yellow highlighter route, red stamps, ransom numbers.
- **Sound:** footstep stamps per step, a passport stamp per app switch, a scissor snip, three clacks.

## C. The aside (the bold one)

**Device:** a character carries the idea; the brand name as the story. **Form:** a paper-theatre stage that rebuilds between acts.

A cut-paper toy theatre. On stage, Sam's work is the play and it must not stop. Sam gets stuck on a line; instead of leaving the stage (the old way: Sam exits, the stage goes dark, the audience waits), a small light appears in the wing at the right edge, the orb, and whispers an aside: the answer arrives in a speech card, Sam delivers the rewritten line, and the play never pauses. Final card: the dictionary entry "a·side: a line spoken off to one side while the scene carries on."

- **First 2 s:** red paper curtains part on a toy stage, Sam at a desk prop, a spotlight cone, the word "Act I · 09:12" on a paper banner.
- **Beats:** curtain → Sam stuck → (old way) Sam walks off, the stage waits, the audience fidgets → rewind → the orb glows in the wing, whispers → the line delivered, the scene carries on → dictionary card → end card.
- **Look:** a toy theatre of layered cut paper, red curtains, a cobalt night backdrop, warm spotlight; ransom-letter banners.
- **Sound:** a curtain swish, a soft theatre murmur, a whisper chime for the aside, applause at the end.
