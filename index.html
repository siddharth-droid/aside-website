a·side noun

In a play, an aside is a line spoken off to one side while the scene carries on.

That's how a quick question should feel. You ask, you get your answer, and the scene, your work, never stops. No new tab, no copying context across, no copying the answer back.

One question, start to finish, in the app you were already in.

  1. Summon

    Press your trigger from any app. It's Alt and Mouse 5 until you pick your own: any key, or a modifier with the middle or side mouse buttons. The orb slides out from the edge.

  2. Hand it the context

    Whatever you had selected comes along, shown in the panel so you can see exactly what was sent. Drop it with one click if you didn't mean it.

  3. Ask

    With a selection, explain, translate and rewrite are one key each. Otherwise, a few words. No describing what's on your screen.

  4. Read

    A short answer, streamed out of the orb, always in the same place at the right edge. The first sentence is the answer; the rest is detail if you want it.

  5. Use it

    Copy it, or replace your selection: the rewrite or translation is pasted into the app you came from, where the original was.

  6. Get back to work

    Esc or the trigger again. The orb tucks back into the edge and you're where you were, with the caret where you left it.

It remembers how you like answers, so you never say it twice.

“I'm on Windows.” “Give me PowerShell.” “Keep it short.” Aside picks these up as you go and keeps them in a small local file, about a page long, that you can read in plain sentences.

Entries carry the date they were last confirmed. Anything contradicted is dropped, anything unused for months lets go. Edit any line, forget it, or turn memory off with one switch.

Aside's servers

None. There aren't any.

It only sees what you hand it.

  • No watching. Aside never looks at your screen on its own: no active-window capture, nothing in the background. A selection, a dragged region or a dropped file is the whole of it.
  • No account. Bring your own keys; they stay in Windows Credential Manager.
  • Nothing kept. A conversation lasts until New chat or until you quit. Memory is the one exception, and you can read every line of it.
What does it cost?

Aside has no subscription. You pay your model provider for what you use, on your own key. Text answers default to DeepSeek; Gemini is a switch in Settings.

Which apps does it work in?

Any app on Windows where you can select text, and anything on screen you can drag the orb over. Replace selection pastes back into the app you came from.

Can it search the web?

Yes, if you add a Tavily key. A “Searching for …” line shows while it looks.

Will it act for me, or click things?

No. It answers, and for how-to questions it lights up where to click. You do the clicking.

Is there a Mac version?

No. Aside is built on Windows itself: its trigger, selection and key storage are Windows parts.

Does it follow dark mode?

Yes. The panel and the orb follow your Windows app mode and switch live when you change it.

Questions, answered short.

Aside is question and answer, nothing else. Not a chat app, not a workspace, not an agent that acts for you.
