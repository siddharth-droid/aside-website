privacy

It only sees what you hand it.

Aside never looks at your screen on its own: no active-window capture, nothing in the background. A selection, a dragged region or a dropped file is the whole of it.

  • Your provider, your key. OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, or local Ollama. One key handles text and captures (with optional separate vision or Tavily web search). Keys stay in Windows Credential Manager.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for, and nothing of yours on our side.
  • Nothing kept. A conversation lasts until New chat or until you quit.
  • Memory you can read. The one exception: a local file in plain sentences, off with one switch.

questions

Questions, answered short.

Aside is question and answer, nothing else. Not a chat app, not a workspace, not an agent that acts for you.

What does it cost?

Aside has no subscription. Bring your own key for OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, or OpenRouter, or run locally with Ollama for free.

Which apps does it work in?

Any app on Windows where you can select text, and anything on screen you can drag the orb over. Replace selection pastes back into the app you came from.

Can it search the web?

Yes, if you add a Tavily key. A “Searching for …” line shows while it looks.

Will it act for me, or click things?

No. It answers, and for how-to questions it lights up where to click. You do the clicking.

Is there a Mac version?

No. Aside is built on Windows itself: its trigger, selection and key storage are Windows parts.

Does it follow dark mode?

Yes. The panel and the orb follow your Windows app mode and switch live when you change it.
